How a project moves

  1. Vision and concept

    We learn your goals, lifestyle, budget and site, then sketch concepts and floor plan layouts to choose from.

  2. Detailed design and plans

    The chosen concept becomes a full set of drawings with dimensions, materials and structural coordination, ready for pricing and permit review.

  3. Permitting and build support

    We help you through permitting and answer contractor questions during construction so the build matches the drawings.
